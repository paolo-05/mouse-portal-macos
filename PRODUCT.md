# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users with non-standard multi-display arrangements, especially professionals who keep deliberate gaps or offsets between displays and still need uninterrupted pointer movement. They use MousePortal briefly to configure connections, then expect it to disappear into the menu bar and remain dependable.

## Product Purpose

MousePortal creates configurable, invisible passages between display-edge segments without changing the arrangement defined in macOS. Success means pointer travel feels continuous and predictable, profiles survive display changes, and day-to-day operation requires no attention.

## Brand Personality

Native, discreet, reliable. The app should feel like a focused macOS system utility: calm, precise, and trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid web-dashboard styling, decorative cards, loud gradients, gamified controls, novelty interactions, and visual effects that distract from spatial configuration. Do not imitate generic cross-platform settings apps or expose implementation details in the primary workflow.

## Design Principles

1. Make spatial relationships visible and directly editable.
2. Prefer familiar macOS controls and behaviors over custom interaction for its own sake.
3. Keep the everyday experience quiet; surface detail only while configuring or diagnosing.
4. Make pointer behavior predictable through proportional mapping, clear previews, and conservative safety defaults.
5. Keep ownership local: no account, network dependency, or telemetry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The first version prioritizes a clear English interface and standard macOS semantics. VoiceOver labels, keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast, non-color-only state cues, and Reduced Motion support are desirable quality goals, but full accessibility conformance is not a release blocker for the initial prototype.
